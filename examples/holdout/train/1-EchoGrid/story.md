**Framing Story**

EchoGrid is a specialized digital workspace designed for acoustic architects who engineer custom resonant chambers for high-precision audio research laboratories. On an ordinary day, an acoustician logs in during short, focused design sessions. They list their own chamber blueprints, open a specific chamber to tweak material absorption coefficients or dimensions, and occasionally switch back to a previously saved chamber project to compare frequency responses.

To maintain strict intellectual property boundaries across shared lab environments, **one acoustician must not be able to modify, overwrite, or delete a chamber blueprint owned by another user**, ensuring design integrity remains strictly isolated to its creator.
