### **Framing Story**

- **Users:** Amateur mycologists who manage small-scale indoor mushroom cultivation setups at home.
- **Ordinary day:** During brief, focused sessions, a cultivator opens SporeNote, lists their active substrate tubs, opens a specific tub to record humidity levels or growth milestones, and occasionally revisits a previously archived tub to review its final harvest yield.
- **Restriction:** User A must never be able to view, edit, or access User B's private substrate tubs or cultivation logs under any circumstances.
