### Framing Story

**Users:** Independent vinyl record designers and custom pressing artists who craft limited-edition physical audio releases.

**Ordinary Day:** During brief studio breaks, a designer opens the application to check their active workspace. They list their current project objects (such as side-A track layouts or label art proofs), open a specific track object to adjust audio runtime parameters or groove spacing, and later return to a previously edited mastering note object to cross-reference equalization settings from an earlier session.

**Access Constraint:** Designer Alice must never be able to view, open, or modify Designer Bob’s unreleased track drafts, master plates, or private project notes.
